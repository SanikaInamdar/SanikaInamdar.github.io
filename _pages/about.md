Hey, I'm Sanika, a backend engineer based in Pune, working where cloud infrastructure meets applied AI.

I'm a Software Engineer at [Espressif Systems](https://www.espressif.com), on the Cloud Backend Engineering team behind [ESP RainMaker](https://rainmaker.espressif.com) and ESP Private Agents. I design, build and own high-throughput services for an enterprise-scale cloud IoT platform — database schemas, async batch pipelines and admin control APIs, written in Go on AWS serverless — and I'm increasingly bringing agentic AI and RAG systems into that stack. I joined Espressif as an Associate Software Engineer in June 2024, and I'm an AWS Certified Developer – Associate.

My interests are distributed systems, system design and scalable backend infrastructure. In my final year at [PICT](https://pict.edu), I co-built a distributed MQTT broker — a load-balanced, Redis-synchronised architecture for large-scale IoT messaging — sponsored and mentored by Golain. The work was published at [IEEE ESCI 2024](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10497427) and won 2nd runner-up at INC 2024 (Impetus and Concepts). On the side, I work through MIT's distributed systems labs (MapReduce, Raft) in Go.

Before that, I interned as a Software Engineer at [Mastercard](https://www.mastercard.com) in the Product & Engineering unit, improving the Regression Monitor platform of Mastercard Payment Gateway Services (MPGS), used by 42+ teams worldwide. At [Azodha](https://azodha.com), I migrated a React website to Next.js for server-side rendering and built MongoDB schemas and Node.js APIs.

I graduated from Pune Institute of Computer Technology in 2024 with a B.E. in Computer Engineering and Honours in AI & ML (CGPA 9.30). I was a senior member of the PICT ACM Student Chapter (PASC), where I led the Web & App Dev event, and a Harvard WECode scholar.

Outside work, I enjoy elocution, portrait sketching, canvas painting, badminton and cricket. I also write about interview experiences on [Medium](https://medium.com/@sanika.inamdar.2002).

Feel free to reach out about backend, distributed systems or cloud IoT — I'd be glad to hear from you.
