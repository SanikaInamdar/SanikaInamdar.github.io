  1. IEEE ESCI
    Distributed MQTT Broker: A Load-Balanced Redis-Based Architecture
    Rohan Doshi, Sanika Inamdar, Tanmay Karmarkar, and others
    Abs URL

    The Internet of Things (IoT) has revolutionized data transfer in industries through the MQTT protocol, a lightweight publish-subscribe messaging protocol. Traditional MQTT brokers are single-node setups, while distributed brokers offer scalability, redundancy, load balancing and fault tolerance for large-scale IoT deployments. The proposed Load-Balancer Redis-based architecture for distributed MQTT brokers overcomes the shortcomings of traditional brokers: the Load-Balancer transfers all incoming TCP packets to the brokers for processing, ensuring equal utilization of each broker node, and Redis acts as a synchronization layer for the brokers where state information is stored.
