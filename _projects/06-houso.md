A housing society management platform with two roles — society admin and flat owner.

- admins add and collect monthly maintenance, and manage flats, owners and tenants (full CRUD)
- flat owners check their dues and pay maintenance online

**Stack:** React.js, Node.js, Express.js, MySQL

code available at <a href="https://github.com/SanikaInamdar/HouSo_React_MySQL_Node">repo</a>
